## Worked well

- SENATE / YOUR DUTIES: the yearly choices (work your bench, committee, read the accounts; the Governor's/Minister's returns; the Treasurer's budget) each state exactly what they do, with numbers. Best-written part of the game.
- The magistrate's business on LADDER AND COURTS: the quaestor's accounts and the praetor's case ("Rule on the law / Bring them to terms / Rule for a friend / Take the gift") are clear, quick and have real consequences (I was removed for skimming, later convicted for a friendly ruling).
- "On the record · Bring the charge": prosecuting a former magistrate is one click with the judge's temper shown ("who is upright, and will go by the record") and a result next year.
- The whip/bribe rows give a live tally ("Fails… for 33 · against 79" to "Carries… for 65 · against 48"), so lobbying feels immediate.
- The Peace of the Reach motion ended a war I could not fight; the consul's veto saved my office; my own motion being vetoed by a rival consul felt fair.
- DYNASTY / MARRIAGES negotiation: wish / favour / family numbers, dowry, children's name, and "they will refuse: the match stands at 16 with them and needs 50" before you ask.
- Disabled buttons nearly always carry a reason on hover; several are exact ("they do not think enough of you (regard 0, needs 10)", "not before 34", "your house has brought a charge this year").
- The Act of the Senate form shows each option's effect in the select; LAWS > THE ESTATES shows who would warm to what.
- Alerts with dates ("You could stand for praetor: the election is in 2 year(s)") and the standing penalty line ("Until year 18 its candidates lose 12 votes at every count").
- The Chronicle, once found, records everything (court rulings, election counts, declarations of war); the person profile explains regard in words.
- Setting a report aside, fixing my purse, and coming back to it in the same turn worked.
- No console errors and no hung turns in 25 years; turns resolve in 4-6 seconds.
