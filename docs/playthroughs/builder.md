## Worked well

- System-panel build lines: every line has yield, upkeep and price (and the years, when more than one), and a disabled button nearly always carries a plain reason ("Needs development 3 (it is 2)", "Needs an Outpost here first", "One such demand a year", "Only at a key of the lanes"). Multi-year works show the first share ("Needs 900 credits, 6 materials to begin").
- The slot breakdown ("3 of 7 slots free · 10 at the most / 2 the world itself / 2 development / 2 the rank of its holder / 1 ground cleared") made slots easy to understand; Clear ground worked first time.
- INFRASTRUCTURE tab: each track says what a level gives, the header (level, "46 of 100 · growing toward 67", slot and building-level thresholds) updates the moment a level is laid.
- Dues controls (forgiven / customary / heavy) rewrite the province's sentence at once and the top bar moves with them; commuting my own dues on Cinder Gate was one click with a clear result ("commuted until year 22").
- Building in a province I hold on another house's world, and in a march I hold on my own world, both worked and changed the province's yield line immediately.
- EXCHANGE > PROJECTS: per site "Paid / Still owed / next year", Pause-Resume, Rush with its premium, Abandon with the refund stated, and a short "HOW BUILDING WORKS".
- EXCHANGE > VENTURES table (cost, built in, earns, pays back, sorted by return) made founding a Skimmer Fleet an easy decision; goods rows explain price moves ("Up 39% on the year: the merchants' stocks are thin").
- Colony flow once in range: one button, "House Castellan has a colony on its way here: 3 year(s) more", then a new holding in the outliner and new ledger lines (tolls, seat, diggings, march).
