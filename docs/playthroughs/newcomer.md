## Worked well

- Custom character creator overall: presets ("The Heir / The Officer / The New Man / The Colossus"), "Throw the dice", save/load a character file, painted faces by ancestry that age with you, a points bar with a running breakdown, clear over-budget message.
- Tooltips on natures, aptitudes and backgrounds (title screen) are short and say what they do.
- Disabled buttons nearly always carry a reason ("not before 25", "needs 1,500 credits of your own", "they do not think enough of you (regard 0, needs 10)", "a patron must be twice as rich as the client").
- The outliner: reports with ✓ when answered, an alert list that links to the window, and the three council one-liners (Chancellor / Steward / Admiral), e.g. "Our rank of Legate outstrips our standing by 11; our clients grumble and rivals take note."
- Every window opens with a plain-English summary sentence and stat tiles with hover text (House: "The house clears 500 a year, the most of it from tolls at Chalke, and is the 9th richest of 32 houses").
- LAWS → THE ESTATES tells you the one thing to do per estate ("would warm to: the tithe → lenient tithe (+15, 1 influence)").
- SENATE → YOUR DUTIES and LADDER AND COURTS: every option states its consequence in words; standing for quaestor, hearing appeals and closing the accounts were easy to follow.
- SENATE → THE CHAMBER: the hemicycle, the predicted count ("Carries as the benches lean · for 93 · against 12"), Vote for / against / Abstain.
- Building descriptions (yield, upkeep, cost, years) and the HOUSE build tooltips with payback ("pays for itself in 2 year(s)"); the PROJECTS tab with Pause / Rush / Abandon.
- HOUSE → THE LEDGER layout (income lines, stores, borrow) and the Exchange's "how a share is priced" notes.
- DYNASTY → MARRIAGES courtship panel: "Asked now, on these terms: they will refuse: the match stands at 22 with them and needs 50."
- CHRONICLE: complete log with house/Reach tabs, category filters and a timeline.
- Reload → "Continue the saved saga" brings the game back; end of turn takes 4–6 s; no console errors in the whole session.
- The Academy's lectures are clear and well written.
- The primer is short and readable; Esc closes windows; number keys 1–9 open rail windows.
