## Worked well

- Laying down a hull is one click, instant, and it joins the squadron already at that world; "Refit … (N materials → readiness 100)" is one click and clear.
- Move orders on the map: with a fleet selected the reachable lanes light up with jump counts ("1j", "2j"), a dashed route is drawn, the outliner shows "→ Cinder Gate" and MILITARY shows "UNDER WAY · 6 JUMP(S)".
- Levies: the year after I declared war the houses holding provinces under me sent hulls on their own ("House Severin sends the levy of The Drydock Nine March to Vane: 1 hull(s) under Marcella Severin, until the peace"); they obey map orders, fought, gained veterancy, and went home at the peace. The enemy got levies the same way.
- Provinces (home-world row on the map): dues forgiven/customary/heavy, "Commute the dues: 600 credits to you now", "Ask them to step down, for your own house", and the "Step down / Stand, and fight" demand from the house above me are all understandable once found.
- Sieges start by themselves on arrival and show a clock ("Siege: Vane · 1/2", outliner "· besieged", alert "(1/4)"); relieving the world by battle lifts it ("The blockade of Pallas Skimmers is lifted").
- MILITARY header forecast when the enemy is alongside: "Battle at Pallas Reach at the year's end against House Meridian: your 2 hull(s) against 4 as reported, odds 63 in a hundred", reacting to the stance I pick.
- Stance / loadout / command-style tooltips state the rock-paper-scissors and the trade-offs plainly ("press: +15% power, more losses; beats the skirmish, beaten by the line"; "the flag: every fleet of the house +3% per point of tactics; never lost with the line").
- The career: Lieutenant → Commander → Admiral → Fleet Admiral over 15 years, epithet "the Victorious", record lines ("Took the Severin standard at Pallas Reach"), renown 10 → 75, the Arms standing breakdown in SENATE > The Orders, "a year ashore" postings (Drill the crews / Patrol / Board of inquiry), and events that fitted the moment ("The Empty Command", "The Admiral's Temptation", "The Siege Bell", "The Envoy").
- Beyond the Reach tab: each nation's habits, payouts ("A fleet of its beaten pays 800 credits, a world taken 1,600"), where its fleets sit, and graded peace buttons that say the war score they want.
- Moving a Commission in the Senate for "Admiral of the Reach" carried and the next year the 4-hull Reach Squadron was mine to order.
- Almost every disabled button has a reason in its tooltip (even when the reason is sometimes the wrong one).
- Borrowing ("Borrow 2,000 credits (owe 2,600, 400 a turn)"), the Exchange's order-weighted price on the Buy/Sell button, and selling helium from my own skimmers (6 units for 5,400 credits) all worked first time.
- No console errors in 21 years (/errors stayed empty); end of turn took 3–6 seconds.
